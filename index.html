Daily Dashboard for iPadOS

Privacy Policy

How Daily Dashboard handles location, news, scripture, translation, and your on-device content — plainly and completely.

Effective 14 November 2026 Developer: Frank Tsang turnout.castles-0u@icloud.com

This Privacy Policy describes how the Daily Dashboard iPadOS app (“we”, “our”, “the App”) collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards information when you use it. We are committed to keeping your personal information private and treating it responsibly. This policy explains what data the App handles, why it handles it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

Location Data

The App uses Apple CoreLocation to determine your approximate current location in order to display accurate local weather, forecasts, and relevant regional news. Location is requested only while the App is in use — never continuously in the background. The App also lets you pick preset cities (e.g., Hong Kong, London, New York) instead of sharing your device location at all.

We do not store your precise GPS coordinates on any server. Coordinates are used transiently to fetch weather and news, and the specific coordinates are never linked to your identity.

News Content

The App retrieves public RSS / Atom news feeds from trusted outlets you or the App’s defaults select (for example RTHK, SCMP, BBC World, NPR, The Guardian, DW, UN News, PBS NewsHour, and CBC News). Only publicly published headlines, summaries, links, and images are fetched. No account, login, or profile is created, so news content is never linked to a specific individual.

Scripture and Bible Content

The App retrieves Bible verse text, chapter data, and passages from public scripture APIs (bolls.life and bible-api.com) to render the Daily Verse card, Poster Studio artwork, and reflection prompts in English and Hong Kong Traditional Chinese. These lookups contain only the requested passage reference (e.g., Psalm 23:1-4 or 詩篇 23:1-4) — never personal information.

Translation Requests

When you switch a news source, headline, summary, ticker item, or full article paragraph between English and Hong Kong Traditional Chinese (繁體中文 (香港)), the text is sent to a third-party translation service (such as Google Translate or MyMemory) to perform the translation. Only the specific text being translated is sent — no account identifiers, device identifiers, contacts, or other personal information is included in these requests.

On-Device Content

The App stores the following locally on your iPad only, and never uploads it to a server:

  • Bible version, language, theme, and display preferences (e.g., which Bible translation you selected, which poster style you prefer).
  • Reflection notes you write about each Daily Verse.
  • Journal entries you record through the Reflection sheet.
  • Cached news headlines, summaries, article images, and translations for faster offline viewing.
  • Weather and clock preferences (e.g., temperature units, world-clock cities, sweep vs. quartz second hand).

Because these items live solely in your device’s local storage, they are not accessible to us, and deleting the App or resetting your device removes them permanently.

3. Data We Do Not Collect

To keep the App lightweight and privacy-respecting, Daily Dashboard does not:

  • Require you to create an account, sign in, or provide an email address, phone number, or payment information.
  • Collect or transmit your name, contacts, photos, messages, browsing history, health data, financial data, or device identifiers for identification purposes.
  • Use App Tracking Transparency or cross-app/device tracking, because the App does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • Sell, rent, or share your personal information with data brokers or advertising networks.

5. How We Store and Secure Information

  • No server-side database. We do not operate or control any server that stores your personal data.
  • Local-only storage. Preferences, reflection notes, journal entries, and cached content remain on your device.
  • Disk cache. Translated headlines, summaries, and article paragraphs may be cached on your device to speed up repeated views. This cache contains only the translated text and expires automatically; it never includes identifiers.
  • No encryption of your notes is performed by us, because we never see them — your device’s own iOS/iPadOS encryption protects local data.

6. Your Choices and Rights

Because we do not collect identifying personal information, your rights under privacy regulations such as the Hong Kong Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (PDPO), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) are limited to the following choices you control directly:

  • Location: You can deny or revoke location access at any time through Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services on your iPad. The App will still work using preset cities.
  • Translation: You can keep news sources in their original language (select 原文 / Show Original) or switch between English and Traditional Chinese at any time.
  • Reflection notes and journal: Delete the App to remove all local notes, journal entries, and caches permanently.
  • Cached content: Delete the App or use iPadOS storage management to clear cached translations and images.
  • No account deletion is needed because no account exists.

If you believe we have mishandled information covered by this policy, you may contact us at turnout.castles-0u@icloud.com and we will respond promptly.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App requires no account creation, sign-in, or personal profile, there is no mechanism through which a child could submit personal information to us. Parents who are concerned about news content or scripture themes can use iPadOS Screen Time and Content & Privacy Restrictions to control which apps and content categories are available.
